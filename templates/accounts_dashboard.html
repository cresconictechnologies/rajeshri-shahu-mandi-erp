How to Import in Tally Prime

Step-by-step 30-second instructions
  1. Open your Company in Tally Prime or Tally ERP 9.
  2. Press Alt + O (Import menu) from Gateway of Tally.
  3. Select Transactions (or Masters).
  4. Choose file format: XML.
  5. Paste or browse to the downloaded .xml file.
  6. Press Enter. Tally will import all vouchers instantly!
💡 Pro-Tip: If Tally reports duplicate entries, select Combine / Ignore Duplicates in import behavior options.
