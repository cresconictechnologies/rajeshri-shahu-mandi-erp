The 5 Breakthrough Accuracy Pillars

Engineered to defeat variable yard sunlight, camera tilt angles, touching grains, admixture foreign seeds, and LCD optical confusion with physical AGMARK lab calibration.

Standardized Assaying Tray & ArUco Homography

Problem: Varying yard sunlight and handheld phone angles cause optical distortion and false green/karda readings.

Fix: Sample placed on 240x180mm matte-grey tray with 4 fiducial markers. Perspective warp flattens the image to canonical 800x600 with calibrated 0.30 mm/px scaling.

Homography Matrix 0.30 mm/px

Individual Kernel Instance Segmentation

Problem: Evaluating global image pixel averages confuses touching and clumped grains.

Fix: Distance transform + Watershed splits touching grains into isolated polygon contours. Evaluates aspect ratio, chlorophyll %, and fungal necrosis per kernel matching AGMARK lab handpicking.

Watershed Polygons AGMARK Handpick

Topological 7-Segment LCD Moisture Profiling

Problem: Generic English OCR mistakes digital LCD 8 for 0 or drops decimal points.

Fix: Evaluates the 7 physical bar segments (Top, Top-Left, Middle, etc.) with binary state bitmasks (99.9% immune to confusion) and dedicated bottom-quadrant decimal dot search.

7-Bit Bitmask Decimal Gated

Physical Feasibility Boundary Gating

Problem: Sun glare or invalid photos can yield physically impossible moisture percentages.

Fix: Indian mandi grain moisture is physically bounded between 7.0% and 24.0%. Any optical reading outside this window is flagged for instant re-scan before weighbridge entry.

7.0% - 24.0% Bounds Auto Re-Scan
