📊 Rate Corridor Governance Principle

Central Accounts retains exclusive authority over daily commodity purchasing corridors:

  • Branch operators can only purchase within the Min Rate and Max Rate limits published by Central Accounts.
  • If market conditions necessitate buying above the corridor, the parchi is flagged as PENDING until Central Accounts enters an approval reason and stamp.
  • All RTGS payments require verification of bank IFSC and entry of the official Bank UTR number before the parchi is closed as settled.
