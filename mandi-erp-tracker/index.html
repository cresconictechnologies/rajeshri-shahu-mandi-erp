Transparent Quality Grading

Scientific 5-Point Quality Assaying

We eliminate guesswork and arbitrary visual deductions with standardized testing methods aligned with AGMARK and APMC grading procedures.

📐

Standardized Sample Tray

Samples are spread on a standardized non-reflective testing tray with reference scaling. This eliminates shadow variance and sunlight glare for fair grain inspection.

Anti-Glare Calibration
🔬

Kernel-by-Kernel Analysis

Digital segmentation evaluates individual grains, accurately identifying pure bold grains, broken seeds, green kernels, and foreign matter according to AGMARK specifications.

Digital Moisture Verification
